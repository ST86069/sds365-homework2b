Image Compression App Mockup

App Description

This application would allow users to upload an image, select a level of compression using the value of \(k\), and view the compressed version of the image alongside the original version.

The application would provide a simple and easy-to-use interface where users can upload their image, choose the desired compression level, and compare the original and compressed images.

App Mockup

The proposed layout of the app has the image upload and compression controls on the left side of the screen. The original and compressed images are displayed side-by-side, to the right of the image upload and compression controls, in order for users to easily compare the effect different compression levels have on image resolution.
